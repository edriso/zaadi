The unmodified Quran corpus and surah metadata were retrieved from
edriso/learn-tajweed through edriso/true-muslim (2026-09-05).
Upstream surah metadata credits the Quran.com API. The corpus SHA256 is pinned.
Whole short surahs retain the original basmala prefix once, without modification.

Hadith: classical Arabic remembrance texts from individually inspected Sunnah.com
pages, attributed in data/sources.json and within the reader. No website translations
or modern commentary are copied. Narration text is ancient public-domain material.
